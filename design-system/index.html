3D viewer

Shaded by default, wireframe and points on request.

Three.js conventions shared by the hero scene, the model viewer and the offline renders.

ShadedMeshStandardMaterial #D6DBE8 (viewer #C9CFE0), roughness .85, flat shading. Ambient .8 + hemisphere 1.2 + key 1.8 + fill .6.
WireframeEdgesGeometry at 28–30°, ink lines at 60–90% opacity over a 5–7% face fill.
Point cloudArea-weighted surface samples (240k in the hero), elevation ramp, additive blending, 2.2 px.
Ground gridGridHelper 36 divisions, #2E3657 / #1F2540, 55% opacity, a hair below the lowest point.
Scan & segmentsScan plane wire at 16% fill / 90% edge; segment tints cycle ink → wire → ink-2 → #8FB7C9.
Damage markersCircle on the face + 1.5× ring, grade colour, mono label above; records panel on the right.

Rules

Do and don't.

Do
  • Keep every section on the drafting grid; alternate ground and surface.
  • Lead with numbers an engineer can check: spans, mesh counts, approvals.
  • Use Anton only for headlines and the wordmark, never for body.
  • Show models shaded first; wireframe and points are modes.
  • Describe projects by type and scale, e.g. "450 m cable-stayed bridge, Korea".
Don't
  • Don't add a teal or blue accent outside the hero wire, scan plane and segment tints.
  • Don't use shadows, gradients on cards, or fills brighter than surface-2.
  • Don't put the wire mesh behind sections below the hero.
  • Don't name client bridges or sites in copy, captions or screenshots.
  • Don't fake density: point clouds are real surface samples or nothing.
